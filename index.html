Sylvain Gaillard

I am a marine biologist studying how environmental conditions shape the physiology and ecology of phytoplankton, particularly toxin-producing species. My research combines laboratory experiments and field observations, spanning cellular responses, species interactions and community dynamics, and the spatial and temporal patterns of harmful algal blooms (HABs) in coastal ecosystems.

I also study the transfer of algal toxins through marine food webs and their fate and effects in marine animals, both during HABs and under controlled laboratory exposure.

Before joining the LEMAR laboratory as an ISblue International Post-Doctoral Fellow, I was a Postdoctoral Investigator in Donald Anderson’s lab at the Woods Hole Oceanographic Institution (WHOI, USA) and a Postdoctoral Research Associate in Kimberly Reece’s lab at the Virginia Institute of Marine Science (VIMS, USA). I received my PhD from Ifremer Nantes and the University of Nantes under the supervision of Philipp Hess and Hélène Hégaret.
